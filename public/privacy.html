Privacy Policy

Testament: The Living Word

Effective Date: October 22, 2026 | Version 2.0.0-beta

1. Our Privacy Pledge

Testament is built with privacy-by-default architecture. We believe your spiritual reflections, prayers, and scripture study habits are deeply personal. We do not sell your data, we do not track you across other apps or websites, and we do not run third-party advertising networks.

2. What Data We Store Locally On Your Device

Many app settings and study records are stored in browser or app storage on this device. Some features send the text you choose to submit to Testament's API and third-party providers; see Network Communications below.

  • Journal entries and saved reflections: These are stored in browser/app storage on this device. If you choose an AI-powered feature, the text you submit for that request is sent to Testament API and forwarded to Google Gemini for processing.
  • Optional Gemini key: If you enter your own key, the app stores it in this device's local storage and sends it in the request header when you use an AI feature. Testament does not store it in a user account.
  • Study Progress & Scripture XP: Completed stories, memorized verses, streak count, and unlocked historical relics.
  • Settings & Preferences: Reading font scale, narration voice choice, audio ambience volume, and selected language (English, Spanish, French).

You can completely wipe this data at any time inside the app via Settings or by clearing your browser/app storage.

3. Network Communications & Cloud Services

Some features communicate with Testament and third-party services. Hosting providers may process technical connection information such as your IP address. When shared storage is configured, Testament stores a one-way hash of the request IP temporarily to enforce request limits.

  • Public Scripture Text: Bible chapters and verses are fetched from public Scripture APIs (bible-api.com and bolls.life) and cached on your device for offline reading.
  • Audio narration: When available, the text you choose to hear is sent to Testament's narration API and its speech provider to generate audio. The API applies request limits and does not require an account.
  • AI features: Your prompt, answer, or scenario text needed for the feature is sent through Testament's API to Google Gemini. Do not include information you do not want processed by these services. Google's handling is subject to its own terms and privacy policy; Testament does not control its retention practices.
  • Daily scenario totals: When the statistics service is enabled, submitted choices are counted in aggregate. A one-way hash of the browser-generated voter ID is kept for up to 400 days to prevent duplicate votes for the same scenario.
  • Reminders: Reminders are scheduled locally by your browser or device when supported. The current app does not register a server push subscription or send a push token to Testament.

4. Third-Party Libraries & Telemetry

Testament does not integrate third-party ad networks, social media trackers, or invasive telemetry frameworks. We do not use Google Analytics or Facebook SDKs inside the app.

5. Children's Privacy

The app has no age gate or account creation. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information through an AI feature, contact us so we can review the request.
